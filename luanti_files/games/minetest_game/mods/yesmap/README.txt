Yes, Map!
========

Tired of not having minimaps in Minetest Game? Don't want to play in creative,
or have to craft a mapping kit? Never fear: "Yes, Map!" is here to unconditionally
enable every player's minimap. It will also enable your radar, though you can
turn that off that if you wish.

Yes, Map! will work on other games based on MTG, as long as those do not differ
meaningfull from MTG "map" mod. Yes, Map! may extend support to other games in
future, but Minetest Game is the most common one that turns it off.
