# Cubeborn

Minecraft-style content for minetest_game servers (tiny SHELTER, SHELTER techage, survival SHELTER):
dimension pockets, the Nether and the End, structures built by admins, Minecraft mobs, villagers with
trading, raids, experience.

Details for each entry are in the sections below.

### Experience (details: section Experience below)

- One counter per player, levels as in Minecraft (level 5 = 55 points, 15 = 315, 30 = 1395; full
  table in section Experience); the bar above the hotbar, `/xp [player]`.
- Earned as orbs: mobs killed by a player, ores, smelting, ripe crops, fishing, breeding, villager
  trades, the ender dragon.
- Gear needs a level: every armour piece, weapon and tool has one (wood 0 ... netherite and elytra
  30, shown in the tooltip); armour above your level comes off, weapons and tools above it do nothing.
- Anvil repairs cost experience (1 point a hammer blow).
- Death costs a quarter of it, dropped as orbs where you died (admins lose nothing).

### Dimensions and biomes

- No new surface biomes: Overworld mobs use the servers' own biomes.
- Nether (own pocket on tiny; the `nether` mod elsewhere): nether wastes, soul sand valley, crimson
  forest, warped forest, basalt deltas.
- End (pocket): the main island with the dragon arena and obsidian pillars, outer islands with chorus.

## Install

1. Copy this `cubeborn` directory into `games/minetest_game/mods/`.
   Game mods are always loaded; no `load_mod_*` lines are needed.
2. Optional settings go to `minetest.conf` (see `*/settingtypes.txt`):
   - `cubeborn.nether = own|external|off` (default: `external` when the nether mod is installed)
   - `cubeborn.end = true|false`
   - `cubeborn.nether_size`, `cubeborn.end_size` (`x,y,z`), `cubeborn.pocket_max_y` (default -1500)
   - `cubeborn.job_budget_ms` (default 15), `cubeborn.natural_extra`, `cubeborn.natural_mods` (mods that
     generate terrain of their own, default `caverealms,riesenpilz`: their nodes do not count as builds)
   - `cubeborn.rare_respawn_hours` (default 24), `cubeborn.elytra_respawn_days` (default 7)
   - `cubeborn.village_size_min`, `cubeborn.village_size_max` (default 8, 20), `cubeborn.difficulty = easy|normal|hard`
3. Before the first start on a live world, check that the pocket area is empty:
   `python3 tools/check_regions.py worlds/<world>/map.sqlite -2032 -2032 -2032 2047 -1500 2047`

## Admin commands (privilege `cb_admin`)

- `/cb_dim find <nether|end>` — list up to 3 free places below -1500 (generates nothing).
- `/cb_dim create <nether|end> [x y z]` — create the pocket at a free place or at the given
  minimum corner. Work runs in the background; progress is reported in chat.
- `/cb_dim info` — pockets and their state.
- `/cb_dim remove <nether|end>` — unregister a pocket; its blocks stay.
- `/cb_spawn <list|structure> [x y z]` — build a structure (see Structures); `/help cb_spawn` names them all.
- `/cb_scatter <structure> <N>` — spread a structure over the map that already exists: every
  1000×1000 square gets N of them (those already there count; squares cut by the map edge get
  their share), spaced out, on free unprotected ground in fitting biomes. Areas the map generator
  has not made yet are skipped, nothing new is generated. `/cb_scatter status`, `/cb_scatter stop`;
  after a restart run it again. Structures appear only through these commands (and End cities when
  the End is created): the pack adds nothing to the generation of new chunks except a few ores.
- `/cb_populate [status|stop]` — fill the map that already exists with every structure at about
  Minecraft's frequency, in one run: creates the End pocket and (without the `nether` mod) the Nether
  pocket when missing, then strongholds in rings around 0,0 (3 at 1280–2816 nodes; more rings where the
  map reaches), then all Overworld structures, rarest first (per 1000×1000: mansion 0.25, ancient city
  0.5, trial chambers 3.4, ocean monument 3.8, village 3.4, outpost 0.76, desert/jungle temple, witch
  hut, igloo 3.8 each, trail ruins 3.4, pale grove 0.5, desert well 1, ruined portal 2.4, shipwreck
  4.3, mineshaft 15.6, ocean ruins 9.8 — attempts; one in a wrong biome is lost, as in Minecraft); on
  servers with the `nether` mod also fortresses (2.2) and bastions (3.2) in its generated caverns.
  Spots follow the world seed, so running it again adds nothing to handled land and fills land
  explored since — rerun it from time to time. Nothing new is generated. While players are online it
  works gently (`cubeborn.populate_budget_ms` 4, a spot per `cubeborn.populate_spot_s` 1 s, a pause of
  `cubeborn.populate_pause_s` 20 s after each structure); with nobody online at full speed; it waits
  while the server lags. After a restart `/cb_populate` continues where it stopped.
  `/cb_scatter` and `/cb_populate` do not run together.
- `/cb_nether_fill <radius> [y] | status | stop` — with the `nether` mod: add fortresses and bastions around
  the admin (or around 0,0 with a given `y`) near one height, so that players at that height meet them: one
  per 432×432 cell, 2 fortresses to 3 bastions. Unlike `/cb_populate` it generates map, but only each site's
  column and its structure's box. Works under the same load governor; after a restart run it again, filled
  cells are skipped. Radius up to 2000.
- `/cb_lamps [status|stop]` — add lamps to Nether fortresses, bastions and End cities built before they got
  them: lanterns (end rods in End cities) in dark places under their ceilings and along their walls. New ones
  are built with lamps. Works under the same load governor; after a restart it goes on by itself. No mob
  spawning depends on this light.
- `/cb_mob <list|mob> [count]` — summon up to 10 Cubeborn mobs in front of the admin (not the ender dragon).
- `/cb_trader` — call the wandering trader.
- `/cb_xp <player> <+N|-N>` — give or take experience points (also for offline players).

A place is refused when it is protected (areas, protector) or contains anything the
map generator does not place (player builds, torches, chests).

## The Nether

- tiny SHELTER (no nether mod): `/cb_dim create nether` builds a 528 x 136 x 528 Nether pocket with
  netherrack caverns, a lava sea, five biomes (wastes, soul sand valley, crimson and warped forests,
  basalt deltas), quartz, nether gold and ancient debris. Players get there through obsidian portals
  (opening 2x3 to 21x21) lit with flint and steel or fire. Overworld distances shrink about 8 times in
  the Nether; a portal is built on arrival when none is near, and the way back reuses the entry portal
  or builds one on free, unprotected ground.
- SHELTER techage (nether mod): its Nether and portals stay; Cubeborn adds quartz ore and ancient
  debris to newly generated parts of it and the Nether blocks without an equivalent there.
- Blocks the server already has (nether mod netherrack, glowstone, basalt, nether bricks) are reused.

## The End

- `/cb_dim create end` builds the End pocket (tiny 640 x 256 x 640, techage 1200 x 256 x 1200): the main
  island with ten obsidian pillars and end crystals, the exit portal frame in the middle, an obsidian
  entry platform, outer islands with chorus beyond `cubeborn.end_outer_r` (tiny 150, techage 400).
  Islands are lit by invisible ambient light, since pockets lie deep underground.
- The bottom 8 nodes of the End are a void that hurts. Within 150 nodes of the centre nobody without
  `cb_admin` can place protection blocks, create areas, set a home or place travelnets or telemosaic
  beacons; building is allowed.
- Nobody without `cb_admin` can claim land anywhere in the End or in the Cubeborn Nether pocket: no
  protection blocks (also not via the protector placer tool) and no areas. The Nether of the `nether`
  mod (techage) keeps its usual rules.
- An End portal opens when twelve End portal frames around a 3x3 hole all hold an eye of ender
  (eye = ender pearl + blaze powder). It leads to the entry platform. The exit portal and the End
  gateways appear after the dragon is killed; the exit portal leads home.

**Ender dragon** (`cb_mobs_dim:ender_dragon`, 200 HP): it appears when a player enters the arena
(128 nodes around the main island) and circles the pillars, healing from the nearest End crystal
(breaking that crystal costs it 10 HP), dives at players, and perches on the exit portal breathing
clouds that hurt (2 HP every 0.5 s, past armour). Projectiles do not hurt it while it perches. It is
saved with the world like any mob; if it is ever lost (cleared objects), a new one with the same HP
appears after a minute with players in the arena. Its death opens the exit
portal, puts a dragon egg on it (every kill) and adds a gateway (up to 20). Four End crystals on
the rim of the exit portal (3 nodes from its middle, one on each side) call it back: the pillars and
their crystals are rebuilt and the exit portal closes until the next kill.

**Shulker** (`cb_mobs_dim:shulker`, 30 HP): clings to a block face, opens when a player is within 16
nodes and shoots homing bullets (4 HP and 10 s of levitation); closed it takes a fifth of the damage.
When its block goes it clings to another face or teleports up to 8 nodes. Drops a shulker shell half of
the time. Shulkers live in End cities.

**Shulker boxes**: shell, chest, shell (in a column) make a box with 27 slots that keeps its contents
when dug or blown up; boxes never go inside boxes. Dye (minetest_game dyes; light blue = blue + white)
colours a box and keeps its contents.

**Elytra** (`cb_items:elytra_wings`): worn in the chest slot of 3d_armor. Jump, then press jump again
in the air to open them. The flight follows Minecraft's (ported from Mineclonia): look down to dive and
gain speed, look up to climb, which costs speed, so a glide always ends lower than it began. Flying
into a wall fast or landing after a long fall hurts; landing, water or taking them off ends the glide.
Each second of flight wears them (432 seconds in all); worn out they no longer open until repaired on
an anvil.

## Structures (`cb_structures`)

Admins with `cb_admin` build structures with `/cb_spawn <structure> [x y z]` (without coordinates: at
the admin's position) and see the list with `/cb_spawn list`. The place is checked first (protection,
player blocks, other structures, the right dimension and height); the build then runs as a background
job and the admin is told when it is done. Chests get their loot when first opened.

Structures with inhabitants refill them while a player is within 48 nodes: one mob per part per
minute up to a number set by the part's size, and only while the part is intact (at least 75 % of its
recorded blocks and all its key blocks in place). A ruined part stays empty for good. The check runs
every 5 s and is capped per pass, so it costs well under 2 ms even with hundreds of parts nearby.
Rare inhabitants (the witch of a hut, the captain of an outpost tower, the evoker and the illusioner of a
mansion hall, the piglin brute of a bastion)
are one per part, and a killed one is replaced only after `cubeborn.rare_respawn_hours` (default 24)
hours of real time; the wait is saved with the structure, so a restart does not shorten it.

**Nether fortress** (`nether_fortress`): bridges, corridors, a lava well, nether wart rooms and blaze
spawner platforms, ported from Mineclonia. On tiny_shelter two are built automatically when the Nether
pocket is created; on servers with the `nether` mod build them with `/cb_spawn nether_fortress` inside
the Nether. At most `cubeborn.fortress_pieces` rooms (default 40). Chests use the `nether_bridge` loot,
blazes come from the spawners (only near players), lava slimes (lava flans without mobs_slimes) live in
intact rooms. Mob spawners
(blaze, cave spider, spider) can be broken only by admins with `cb_admin`; for everyone else they are
protected, blast-proof and piston-proof.

**Bastion remnant** (`bastion`, kinds `treasure`, `units`, `stable`, `bridge`): as in Bedrock one of four
kinds - the treasure bastion assembled from Mineclonia's templates (ramparts, a lava basin with magma cube
spawners, weathered bricks, the treasure room), or housing units, a hoglin stable or a bridge with a keep.
On tiny_shelter two are built automatically when the Nether pocket is created (in the quarters the
fortresses leave free); elsewhere admins build them with `/cb_spawn bastion` (or `bastion:stable` and so
on) inside the Nether. Piglins live in every part, hoglins in the stable; the **piglin brute** is a rare
inhabitant (one per part, back a day after it is gone). Chests use Bedrock's bastion loot (treasure,
other, bridge, hoglin stable - what the server has). New blocks: cracked polished blackstone bricks,
gilded blackstone, lanterns (stand or hang; steel ingot, torch, steel ingot) and chains (two steel
ingots make three).

**Shipwreck** (`shipwreck`, kinds `ocean`, `beached`): Mineclonia's five wrecks (whole, damaged, half).
On the sea floor (the water at least 10 deep over the whole wreck, which is full of water) or, given on land, beached: sunk a
quarter of its height into the ground. The hull is of one random server wood and the trim of another.
Up to three chests: supply, map and treasure (Mineclonia's loot; the treasure map comes with the maps
cycle). `/cb_spawn shipwreck` picks the kind by the water at the position; `/cb_scatter shipwreck N`
puts them in the open sea.

**Ocean ruins** (`ocean_ruin`, kinds `cold`, `warm`): Mineclonia's templates. Cold ruins are stone bricks,
half of them mossy and many cracked; warm ones sandstone, chosen by the biome's heat (50 or more is warm).
Three in ten are big, and a big one usually has 4-7 small ruins around it, each on its own sea floor. Sand
and gravel fall to the ground, the insides are full of water. Chests use Bedrock's underwater ruin loot
(small or big); drowned live in intact big ruins. The water must be as deep as the ruin is tall.

**Ruined portal** (`ruined_portal`, kinds `standard`, `desert`, `jungle`, `swamp`, `mountain`, `ocean`,
`nether`): Mineclonia's five small and three giant portals (one in twenty is giant) with its setups: on
the surface or underground (standard, mountain), partly buried (desert), overgrown with leaves and vines
(jungle), on the swamp or sea floor, and in the Nether of polished blackstone. A third of the gold blocks
are gone, stone bricks are mossy or cracked, some obsidian is crying, netherrack and magma spread over the
ground around and hang under the floor. One chest with Bedrock's ruined portal loot. Fill the gaps of the
frame with obsidian (the corners too) and light it. With the `nether` mod (ta, survival), which lights
only 4×5 frames, every ruined portal is built with a broken 4×5 frame; on tiny_shelter the frames keep
Mineclonia's sizes (4×5 to 7×7). `/cb_spawn ruined_portal`
picks the kind by the place (the Nether, water, the biome, above y 60 the mountain kind).

**Stronghold** (`stronghold`): corridors, stairs, crossings, prison cells, libraries and the End portal
room with twelve frames (each holds an eye with a 10 % chance), ported from Mineclonia. Only in the
overworld between heights -1499 and -20; `/cb_spawn stronghold` buries it with its top one node under
the given position (under -20 when given from above). At most `cubeborn.stronghold_pieces` pieces
(default 50). Chests use the stronghold corridor, crossing and library loot; stone monsters live in
intact rooms. End portal frames cannot be crafted (as in Minecraft): the way to the End is through a
stronghold, so admins spread them with `/cb_scatter stronghold N` (they go 30 nodes under the ground)
and players find one with an eye of ender. The tooltips of the ender pearl, blaze rod and powder, eye
of ender and portal frame tell the chain.

**End city** (`end_city`): towers, bridges, houses and sometimes a ship, assembled from Mineclonia's
End city templates. Up to `cubeborn.end_cities` (default 3) are built on the outer islands when the End
pocket is created; admins add more with `/cb_spawn end_city` inside the End. Shulkers live in them,
chests hold End city loot, and the ship's elytra stand on an armour stand (in a chest without
3d_armor_stand). Purple stained glass and the dragon head come with them. The ship's elytra come back on
their stand `cubeborn.elytra_respawn_days` (default 7) days of real time after they were taken (a
broken stand is put back; something else left on the stand is never replaced).

**Village** (`village`, variants `plains`, `desert`, `savanna`, `taiga`, `snowy`): `/cb_spawn village`
picks the variant from the biome, `/cb_spawn village:taiga` forces one. A belltower in the middle,
8-20 buildings from Mineclonia's village schematics (houses, farms, workshops, lamps) on foundations
that follow the ground, gravel paths to the centre, Bedrock village chest loot. Buildings that would
touch water, a cliff, protection or a player's build are left out; with no room for the centre and
the minimum (two houses, a farm, a well) the command refuses. Villagers live there (one per bed,
each sleeps in its own bed at night and goes home when the bell rings) with an iron golem; both come
back while the houses and their beds stand. Zombie villagers turn villagers they kill (by
`cubeborn.difficulty`: never on easy, half on normal, always on hard). Players build iron golems
from four iron blocks and a pumpkin. Doors zombies break come back the next morning if nothing took
their place.

**A villager's day.** From morning (0.23) a villager with a profession walks to its job site and works
there for 2-5 minutes at a time, now and then spending a minute at the bell; idlers (no job, nitwits,
children) go out for a walk in daylight near the bell for 1.5-4 minutes, come back home for 1-2, and go
out again. In the evening (0.79) everyone walks back to its bed. On the way villagers open plain
wooden doors (any mod's `doors` door) and shut those they opened once through, unless someone stands
in the doorway; iron, glass and obsidian doors, gates and trapdoors stop them. Raiders open wooden
doors during a raid, and so does the wandering trader; ravagers and mobs outside a raid do not. Only
zombies ever break doors.

**Players' houses and villages.** A bed with a wooden door within 8 nodes, up to 32 nodes outside a
generated village's area, is a house of that village: villagers sleep and breed there, it counts for
the golems, raids and the wandering trader. A **bell** a player places farther away (not within 32 of
a village's area nor 64 of another player's village bell) founds a **player's village**: 32 nodes
around the bell, 16 up and down. Its houses are its beds with a wooden door within 8. Villagers brought
there live and breed in it, an iron golem (one per 10 beds, at least one) comes to the bell, Bad Omen
starts a raid on it and the wandering trader visits it, as in a generated village. Nobody else moves
in: only the villagers brought there and their children live in it. Breaking the bell ends the village
(a raid on it stops); the villagers stay but do not breed until a bell is back. Outside villages
villagers do not breed, as in Minecraft. A generated village stays a village even without its bell.

**Taking villagers away.** A villager on a lead (the servers' `leads` mod) follows it and does not try to
go home, breed or trade. A moving boat or cart that comes within 1.5 nodes of a villager (adult or child,
awake) takes it in: a boat seats two, the villager at the back and the player steering at the front; a
cart seats one, so push it along the rails. The villager gets out when the boat or cart is broken or
unloaded, or when a player right-clicks it. Let go more than 16 nodes from its bed, a villager forgets
the bed and takes the nearest free one; more than 48 away it forgets it anyway, and a job site more than
32 from the new bed is given up too. Iron golems do not ride.

**Abandoned mineshaft** (`mineshaft`, not above -20): Mineclonia's corridors, crossings and stairs
around a dirt parlor at the given height, at most `cubeborn.mineshaft_pieces` (60) pieces, rails,
loot chests where minecart chests would be, cobwebs and a cave spider spawner. Cave spiders are
small and poison (7 s on normal, 15 s on hard).

**Ancient city** (`ancient_city`, deep under the ground): see The deep dark.

**Trial chambers** (`trial_chambers`, under the ground): see Trial chambers.

**Pale grove** (`pale_grove`, on the surface): see Pale groves and creakings.

## Beacon, conduit, respawn anchor (`cb_beacon`)

- **Beacon** (glass ×5, nether star, obsidian ×3) on a pyramid of 1–4 levels of iron, gold, diamond,
  emerald or netherite blocks (also mese and moreores silver/mithril blocks), the sky open above (glass
  lets the beam through). Paid once with an iron or gold ingot, a diamond, an emerald or a netherite
  ingot, it gives players within 20/30/40/50 nodes the chosen power every 4 s for 11 s: speed or haste
  (level 1), resistance or jump boost (2), strength (3); at level 4 also regeneration or the primary
  power at level II. Emerald and netherite blocks: 9 gems or ingots.
- **Haste** speeds up digging with the wielded tool (+20 % a level); the tool gets its own caps back
  (toolranks level, enchantments) as soon as it is put away or the effect ends.
- **Conduit** (heart of the sea, nautilus shell ×8) in water, inside a frame of prismarine, prismarine
  bricks, dark prismarine or sea lanterns (the rims of the three 5×5 squares through it, 42 places):
  16+ blocks wake it up, every 7 reach 16 nodes further (max 96). Swimmers in reach get Conduit Power
  (breath, light, faster digging under water); a full frame hurts one monster in the water within 8
  every 2 s. The heart of the sea lies in every shipwreck treasure chest and in some big ocean ruin chests.
- **Respawn anchor** (crying obsidian ×6, glowstone ×3) is charged with glowstone (up to 4). In the
  Nether a click makes it your respawn point; each respawn costs a charge, an empty or broken anchor
  gives the normal respawn back. Used anywhere else it explodes (in protected places it only hurts).

## Campfire and scaffolding (`cb_blocks`)

- **Campfire** (three sticks around coal or charcoal over three logs; soul campfire: soul sand or soil):
  placed lit. Right-click raw food on it: up to four cook at once, 30 s each, no fuel, and pop out
  cooked. Standing in it burns (1 a second, soul 2). Smoke rises (higher over a hay bale). A shovel or a
  water bucket puts it out; flint and steel or a fire charge lights it again. Broken it drops charcoal
  (soul: soul soil); with Silk Touch itself.
- **Scaffolding** (6 bamboo and a string → 6): climb it like a ladder (jump up, sneak down). On a solid
  block, on top of scaffolding, or up to 6 sideways from a supported one; using scaffolding on the top

## Bundle and lodestone (`cb_items`)

- **Bundle** (string over leather; dye it in 15 colours, the contents stay): holds mixed items up to a
  weight of 64 (an item weighs 64 / its stack size, a tool 64, a bundle 4 plus its contents; no shulker
  boxes). In the inventory drop an item onto the bundle to put it in, or the bundle onto an item to
  pick it up. Using it drops out the last stack; sneak + use opens a window to take out any stack.
- **Lodestone** (8 stone bricks around a netherite ingot): use the compass on it and that compass points
  to the lodestone (in the same dimension; it spins elsewhere). Of a stack only one compass is bound.
  When the lodestone is gone the compass leads to your last death again.

## Fireworks (`cb_items`)

- **Firework star**: gunpowder + 1–8 dyes, optionally one shape item (fire charge: large ball, gold
  nugget: star, a skull or head: creeper face, feather: burst; none: small ball), a diamond (trail) and
  glowstone dust (twinkle). A star with 1–8 dyes gets fade colours. The tooltip lists it all.
- **Rocket**: paper + 1–3 gunpowder (flight duration) + stars → 3 rockets. Used on a block it flies up
  for Minecraft's time (10·(flight+1) + 0–11 ticks) and bursts into its stars; a ceiling stops it at
  once. Used while gliding with elytra it boosts the flight for the same time (up to ~34 nodes/s, as
  in Minecraft); stars on a boosting rocket burst around the flyer. Bursts hurt players within 5 nodes:
  (5 + 2 per star) × sqrt((5 − distance)/5). Players have no crossbows, so rockets are never shot.
- **Fire charge** (blaze powder, coal, gunpowder → 3) sets fire where it is used (it lights Nether
  portals like flint and steel) and gives the large-ball shape.
- **Gold and iron nuggets**: 9 make an ingot and back; loot tables hand them out.

## Effects (`cb_core.effects`)

Poison (1 damage every 1.25 s, never below 1 HP), slowness (speed x0.85 per level), weakness (half
damage to mobs), Bad Omen and Hero of the Village (raids), darkness (a pulsing vignette; sculk
shriekers). They show as icons in the top right corner and survive leaving the game.

Potions add speed, jump boost, strength (+3 damage per level), resistance (−20 % damage per level),
fire resistance, water breathing, night vision, invisibility (no model and name tag; mobs do not go for
the player unless hit by them, the warden still hears), slow falling and the 1.21 effects: infested
(silverfish when hurt), oozing (slimes), weaving (cobwebs) and wind charged (a gust) on death. Death
clears all effects; milk (a bucket or glass from mobs_animal) clears them too.

## Potions and brewing (`cb_potions`)

The **brewing stand** (blaze rod over three cobblestone; village clerics' stands work too) takes three
bottles, one ingredient and blaze powder for fuel (one powder = 20 brews); a brew takes 20 s. Fill a
glass bottle at a water source, then: nether wart → awkward potion; awkward + an ingredient → a potion
(glistering melon: healing, golden carrot: night vision, sugar: swiftness, magma cream: fire
resistance, blaze powder: strength, pufferfish: water breathing, ghast tear: regeneration, spider eye:
poison, rabbit's foot: leaping, phantom membrane: slow falling, turtle shell: the Turtle Master, stone:
infestation, slime block: oozing, cobweb: weaving, breeze rod: wind charging). **Etherium dust**
(ethereal) makes a potion last longer (it stands for redstone), **glowstone dust** makes it stronger
(II); the **fermented spider eye** (spider eye, brown mushroom, sugar) turns a drinkable potion into
its opposite. **Gunpowder** makes a useful potion a splash one, **dragon's breath** a splash potion a
lingering one. A harmful potion (harming, poison, weakness, slowness, infestation, oozing, weaving)
turns splash only with an **explosive charge** (gunpowder + ghast tear). Every potion stacks one.
New drops: spider eyes from spiders, rabbit's feet (1 in 10) from rabbits, turtle shells (1 in 10) from
turtles. The craft guide lists every brew under "Brewing".

**Brewing needs experience**; drinking and throwing do not. The player who puts the ingredient in must
have the level of every potion the brew makes, checked at each brew (admins brew anything):

| Level | Brews |
|---|---|
| 0 | water bottle, awkward, mundane, thick; healing, swiftness, night vision, water breathing, leaping |
| 5 | fire resistance, regeneration, strength, slow falling, invisibility; harming, poison, weakness, slowness; long potions of level 0 |
| 15 | II (glowstone) potions; long potions of level 5; splash potions |
| 20 | the Turtle Master; wind charging, weaving, oozing, infestation; lingering potions |
| 25 | strength II, regeneration II, the Turtle Master II |
| 30 | every harmful splash and lingering potion |

A thrown potion's level is the highest of its drinkable base and the throwing step.

**A brew costs experience points**, once for the whole stand (up to three bottles): the price of its
dearest potion, shown in the potion's tooltip. Each kind has its own price: 1 (water, awkward, mundane,
thick), 2 (water breathing, night vision), 3 (swiftness, leaping), 4 (healing, weakness), 5 (fire
resistance, slow falling, slowness), 6 (invisibility, poison), 8 (strength, regeneration, harming), 10
(wind charging, weaving, oozing, infestation), 12 (the Turtle Master). A long potion costs half as much
again, II twice as much, a splash one 5 more (a harmful splash one 50 more), a lingering one another 15;
at most 80 (lingering harming II). The brew starts only if the brewer has the points; they are taken
when it is done, and without them by then nothing is brewed and the ingredient stays (admins pay
nothing).

Splash and lingering potions are thrown with a click: they break on the first block or creature and
splash everyone within 4 nodes (the closer, the more of the time; instant health and damage reach
mobs too, undead the other way round). A lingering potion leaves a cloud for 30 s that shrinks and
gives a quarter of the time to whoever stands in it. With PvP off a potion reaches no other player,
only the thrower.

## Enchanting (`cb_enchanting`)

The **enchantment table** is xdecor's own table (same recipe; tables already in the world work the new
way). Put a tool, a weapon, a piece of armour or a book in it: it shows three offers like Minecraft
Bedrock, stronger with more **bookshelves** around it (two nodes away, at the table's height or one above,
with air in between; at most 15). Offer N costs **N mese crystals** and **N experience levels**, and needs
the level shown on it (1 to 30). The offers stay the same until you enchant something. Admins pay no
experience. A book becomes an **enchanted book**.

Enchantments are stored in the item, which keeps its name: **toolranks levels, the dig counter and the
level bonus stay**, and the tool goes on levelling. Efficiency adds +0.1 speed per level on top of the
level bonus, but a tool never gets faster than **x2.5** (a level-80 tool with Efficiency V); Unbreaking
multiplies durability by its level + 1; Sharpness adds 1.25 damage per level. Old xdecor enchanted tools
turn into the plain tool with Efficiency II, Unbreaking II or Sharpness II.

The table can also roll Fortune, Silk Touch, Smite, Bane of Arthropods, Knockback, Fire Aspect, Looting,
the armour protections, Thorns, Respiration, Feather Falling, Depth Strider, Luck of the Sea and Lure;
their effects, Mending and the curses (no bow, crossbow or trident enchantments: players have no ranged
weapons). Enchanted books combine with items on the anvil, the grindstone takes enchantments off, chests
of structures hold enchanted books and gear, librarians sell books.

## Archaeology (`cb_archaeology`)

Suspicious sand and gravel lie in desert temples, desert wells and ocean ruins (cold ruins: gravel, warm
ones: sand). Punch one with a **brush** (feather over a copper ingot over a stick, 64 uses): four strokes
uncover what it hides — pottery sherds, emeralds, tools, a sniffer egg in warm ruins — and the block turns
plain; left alone for 5 s the brushing goes back. Dug without a brush it gives nothing. 23 pottery sherds
exist.

**Decorated pot**: four sherds or clay bricks in a diamond (top → back, sides → sides, bottom → front);
each side shows its sherd. Right-click puts one item in per click — one kind, up to a stack. Dug, the pot
drops whole with the same pictures and its contents spill out.

**Trail ruins** (`/cb_spawn trail_ruins`, `/cb_scatter` in taiga, jungle, birch and pine forests): buried
terracotta towers, halls and roads 15 below the surface (only the tower top may show), with suspicious
gravel — common finds (dyes, bricks, clay, candles, seeds, emeralds…) and rare ones (seven sherds and the
wayfinder, raiser, shaper and host templates). Nobody lives there.

**Armour trims**: 18 smithing templates (sentry, dune, coast, wild, tide, ward, vex, rib, snout, eye,
spire, silence, wayfinder, raiser, shaper, host, bolt, flow) lie in chests of the structures they belong to
(desert temple, jungle temple, outpost, shipwreck, mansion, stronghold, fortress, bastion, End city,
ancient city, trial vaults) or under suspicious gravel of trail ruins; the elder guardian drops tide (1 in 5). Seven diamonds, a template and its
block (cobble, sandstone, prismarine, deepslate, netherrack, blackstone, end stone, purpur, baked
clay, copper block, breeze rod) make two. At the smithing table template + armour piece + material
(steel, copper, gold, diamond, emerald, netherite, quartz, mese crystal, obsidian shard) give the
same armour with a coloured pattern — on the player, on the armour stand, as a mark on the icon and
in the description. Wear and enchantments stay; a new trim replaces the old one. Shields and elytra
take no trims.

## Illagers (`cb_mobs_illager`, needs mobs_redo)

Pillagers shoot crossbow bolts (entities only: players never get crossbows or bows), vindicators hit
hard and may drop an emerald, evokers call fangs and summon vexes (at most 8 around) and always drop a
totem of undying. Vexes fly through walls and wither away after 30-119 s. Illagers attack players,
villagers and golems, never each other.

**Totem of undying** (`cb_items:totem`): anywhere in the hotbar, a lethal blow leaves 1 HP, the totem
is used up and the player heals for 5 s. **Emerald** (`cb_items:emerald`) drops from illagers.

The **ravager** (100 HP, a blow of 12 that throws its target back) comes with raids only, without
riders; it tramples leaves and crops in front of it where nobody protects them.

## Trading (`cb_mobs_village`)

A jobless villager takes a free job site within 32 of its bed and gets its profession: composter —
farmer, barrel — fisherman, loom — shepherd, fletching table — fletcher, lectern — librarian,
cartography table — cartographer, brewing stand — cleric, blast furnace — armorer, smithing table —
toolsmith, grindstone — weaponsmith, smoker — butcher, cauldron — leatherworker, stonecutter — mason,
and an ATM (`atm` + `currency`) — banker. Without its job site it is jobless again until it has
traded once; nitwits (5 %) never work. A profession with no novice trade on the server is not given.

Right click opens the trade window: offers from Bedrock's tables (`tools/gen_trades.py`; items the
server lacks drop out, ranged weapons always, enchanted goods are sold plain until enchanting
exists), two pay slots and the result. Villagers level up from novice to master, restock up to twice
a day at their job site, raise prices with demand; Hero of the Village gives 30 % off plus 6.25 % per
level above I. A villager trades with one player at a time; pay left in the window returns to the
player when it closes (also at shutdown). The banker gives `cubeborn.bank_sell` (10) minegeld for an
emerald, at most `cubeborn.bank_daily` (16) emeralds per player per game day (20 minutes of game
time: sleeping does not reset it), and sells emeralds at `cubeborn.bank_buy` (`25:2`), taking only
the notes needed and giving change.

Villagers keep a food stock (bread, carrots, potatoes, beetroots; farmers also wheat and seeds):
they pick up dropped food, farmers bake 3 wheat into bread, and a villager with more than it needs
throws the rest to a neighbour short of it (within 8 nodes). Two villagers with 12 food points each (bread 4, the rest 1),
by day and with a free bed in their village (never outside villages), have a child after 5 s
of hearts; the parents rest 5 minutes. Children are half size, run faster, do not trade or work and
grow up after 20 minutes of game time. Farmers harvest ripe wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroots
within 16 of their bed and replant them. The structure engine refills a village with villagers
only while it has fewer than two; the rest comes from breeding.

## Workstations (`cb_blocks`, `cb_items`)

The villagers' job sites work for players too. The **composter** takes organic items with Bedrock's
chances (seeds and leaves 30 %, cactus and sugar cane 50 %, crops and flowers 65 %, bread 85 %, pies
100 %), fills in 7 layers and gives bone meal; a hopper above fills it, one below takes the bone
meal. The **barrel** holds 27 stacks. The **smoker** (food only) and **blast furnace** (ores and
metal only) cook the server's furnace recipes twice as fast. The **grindstone** joins two worn copies
of a tool (both durabilities plus 5 %). The **stonecutter** turns one stone block into a stair, two
slabs, a wall or a carved variant. The **lectern** holds a written book anyone can read; whoever may
build there takes it back. The **smithing table** turns diamond tools and armor plus a netherite
ingot into **netherite** gear (longer-lasting, faster, harder-hitting; the armor resists fire and,
as a full set, lava; it does not burn in lava), keeping enchantments and trims; with a template in
its slot it puts armour trims on (see Archaeology). The cauldron is the server's (xdecor); the
cartography and fletching tables are decoration; the brewing stand brews (see Potions). Workstations obey area
protection.

## Banners (`cb_banners`)

Six wool of a colour and a stick make a banner (one per wool colour of the server). Banners stand
in 16 directions or hang on walls; the picture is one entity per banner. The **loom** adds a layer:
a banner and a dye give a choice of about 30 patterns; a pattern item (paper and a white dandelion,
bricks or a vine) gives its own (flower, field masoned, bordure indented) and is kept. Six layers
at most. Two banners of a colour, one patterned, make two copies in the crafting grid. A patterned
banner used on a water cauldron loses its top layer; three washes empty the cauldron. Pillager
captains carry the ominous banner on their back and drop it when they die.

## Wandering trader (`cb_mobs_trader`, needs mobs_redo)

Every 20 minutes there is a chance (2.5 %, +2.5 % after each miss up to 7.5 %) that a wandering
trader comes to a random player under the open sky: to the bell when the player is in a village,
else 24-48 nodes away. One at a time on the server; it leaves after 40-60 minutes with its two
llamas. It trades Bedrock's wandering trader offers in the villagers' window (no levels, no
restock), turns invisible at night and runs from monsters. Its llamas follow it on leads and spit
at whoever hits them or the trader; take a lead off (with the leads mod; else right-click a llama
empty-handed near the trader) and that llama stays as an ordinary one. Admins: `/cb_trader` calls
the trader to you.

Llamas have no natural spawn: they come with the trader and from breeding. Tame one by riding it
(empty hand); it throws you off until it gets used to you, and wheat or a hay bale helps. Tamed
adults fed a hay bale breed; the young grow up in 20 minutes. A tamed llama wears a carpet and
carries a chest (3 slots per strength 1-5; sneak + right-click opens it, owner only); everything
drops when it dies. You can ride it but not steer it (sneak gets off). Lead one with a lead and up
to nine tamed llamas within 8 nodes follow it in a caravan.

## Natural spawning (`cb_spawning`)

Cubeborn mobs that live in the wild come through one spawn engine: each mob has a rule (dimension,
light, open sky, the node it stands on or swims in, biome words, heights, weight, group size), and two
ABMs (ground and water, every 30 s, rare per node) pick a mob among the rules that fit the spot.
Monsters need full darkness, as in Minecraft. Within 32 nodes of the spot at most
`cubeborn.spawn_cap_monster` (8), `_animal` (6), `_water` (5), `_ambient` (3) Cubeborn mobs of that
category may live; `cubeborn.spawn_chance_scale` (1) makes the ABMs rarer. The mobs_redo settings
apply: `mobs_spawn = false` stops it, nothing spawns within `mob_nospawn_range` of a player, monsters
never in peaceful mode or (with `mobs_spawn_protected = false`) in protected areas.

## Overworld monsters (`cb_mobs_overworld`, needs mobs_redo)

**Zombies** come out in full darkness (caves, nights) and burn in daylight; 5 % are babies that never
grow up and run faster. Villagers they kill become zombie villagers. In deserts most of them are
**husks**, which do not burn and whose blows cause **Hunger** (the `stamina` food bar drops by one every 15 s per level). Both
drop rotten flesh (food; 80 % chance of Hunger for 30 s). Zombies and husks break plain wooden doors
(about 12 s of blows, the door drops as an item) and glass (about 3 s, no drop) that stand between them
and their target; never steel, glass or owned doors, obsidian glass, or anything in protected areas or
in pocket shells. **Drowned** live in dark rivers and seas, do not burn, and 15 % of them throw a
trident (a projectile only; no trident ever drops).

**Creepers** walk up and explode after 1.5 s: the blast hurts and pushes creatures within 3 nodes and
never breaks a node. Lightning (tiny) charges creepers within 4 nodes of the strike: radius 6. They
drop gunpowder. **Spider jockeys** (rare, only with `mobs_monster` and `mobs_skeletons`) are a
skeleton archer riding a spider. **Strays** are the skeletons of snowy biomes: their arrows (mob
projectiles only) hurt 3 and slow for 30 s; they burn in daylight and drop bones. **Silverfish** hide
in infested stone, cobble and stone bricks (strongholds, a little in high mountain stone), come out when
the block is dug or blown up, wake up to four more when hurt, and spawn from a spawner in the stronghold
portal room. **Endermites** come with 5 % of landed ender pearls and live two minutes. **Phantoms** come
at night under the open sky for players who have not slept for 3 game days (sleeping in a bed or dying
resets it): 1-3 at a time, at most 5 around, circling high and swooping; they burn by day and drop
phantom membranes. A membrane crafted with elytra mends a quarter of their durability.

## Animals (`cb_mobs_animals`, needs mobs_redo)

**Pigs** live on grass in daylight, eat carrots, potatoes and beetroots (breeding) and drop raw pork.
A right-click with a saddle (`mobs:saddle`) saddles an adult pig; right-click again to get on, and
once more to get off. A ridden pig moves only while its rider holds a **carrot on a stick** (shapeless:
the ethereal fishing rod + a carrot; no recipe without them); using the stick while riding gives three
seconds of extra speed and wears it (25 uses). A dead pig drops its saddle.

**Mooshrooms** (red, 1 in 10 brown) live on mushroom dirt of ethereal's mushroom biome. A bowl gives
ethereal mushroom soup; shears turn an adult into a plain cow (`mobs_animal`) and five mushrooms of
its colour. They eat wheat.

**Donkeys** live in grasslands and savannas. Feed one wheat, apples or carrots to tame it; then its owner
can saddle and ride it (it steers like a horse) and give it a chest: sneak + right-click opens 15 slots.
A donkey and a horse (`mob_horse`) in love make a **mule**, which rides and carries a chest the same way
but never breeds. A dead donkey or mule drops its saddle, chest and everything in it.

**Skeleton horses** and **zombie horses** come rarely at night. They do not burn, eat or breed and need
no taming: whoever saddles one owns it and rides it (no chest).

**Wolves** live in forests, taigas, snowy lands and savannas, in six coats by biome. Wild ones leave
players alone until hit (then the pack fights back) and hunt skeletons. A bone (`bonemeal:bone`) tames
one with a 1 in 3 chance: a tamed wolf wears a red collar, has 20 HP, follows its owner (and comes to them
from more than 24 nodes away), fights monsters except creepers and attacks whoever hits its owner. The
owner's right-click with an empty hand makes it sit or stand up; a sitting wolf stays put. Raw meat,
pork or chicken heals and breeds it.

**Armadillos** live in savannas and roll up when a sprinting or riding player, their attacker or an undead
mob comes within 4 nodes; rolled up they take half damage minus one. Every 5-10 minutes an adult sheds an
**armadillo scute**. Six scutes make **wolf armor**: the
owner puts it on a tamed wolf with a right-click, it takes the wolf's damage until it breaks after 64,
and shears take it off whole. The servers have no spider eyes, so armadillos do not breed.

**Foxes** (white in snowy biomes) live in taigas and snowy lands. They sleep by day (a blow, or a player
closer than 3 nodes who does not sneak, wakes them), keep away from players and hunt chickens at night.
A fox picks up one item lying near it and carries it in its mouth until it dies. Berries breed them.

**Ocelots** live in jungles, run from players and hunt chickens. Raw fish wins one's trust with a 1 in 3
chance: then it stops running and is never removed; more fish breeds it.

**Parrots** (five colours) live in jungles and fly. Any seeds tame one (1 in 3); a cookie kills it. A tamed
parrot sits on its owner's shoulder (two at most) when they come close and flies off when they jump, get
hurt, mount something or leave; now and then it mimics a monster nearby.

**Goats** live high up (y 60 and more) on snow, stone and grass. Every few minutes a goat rams the nearest
player (not in creative) or mob other than a goat within 8 nodes; a charge that ends against stone, a
tree or ice knocks off one of its two horns. A **goat horn** plays its call far and wide on right-click
(8 calls, 7 s apart); 2 goats in 100 are screamers that ram more often and whose horns play the other four calls. An
empty bucket gives milk (`mobs:bucket_milk`); wheat breeds goats.

**Frogs** come in three kinds by the heat of the biome (temperate, warm, cold) near water in swamps,
jungles and woods. A frog's tongue catches small slimes (`mobs_slimes`) within 3 nodes; a small lava slime
becomes a **froglight** of the frog's kind (ochre, pearlescent, verdant: full light). The servers have no
slimeballs, so frogs do not breed: **tadpoles** come by themselves in warm shallow water and grow into the
frog of their climate after 20 minutes. A water bucket takes a tadpole (and keeps its age); placing the
**bucket of tadpole** pours the water and lets it out.

**Axolotls** live in dark underground water, in six colours and a rare purple. They fight drowned; when
their foe dies, players within 20 nodes get a few seconds of regeneration. Badly hurt, an axolotl plays
dead for 10 s and heals. Out of water it dries out after 5 minutes. A water bucket takes one (bucket of
axolotl, keeps its colour); caught tropical fish or clownfish (`mobs_fish`) breed them.

**Polar bears** live on ice and snow and fight back as a group; a grown bear with a cub nearby attacks
players who come within 6 nodes. One in ten is a cub. They drop raw cod or salmon and cannot be bred.

**Camels** live in deserts and need no taming: a saddle makes its owner the driver, and anyone may sit
behind (right-click; again to get off). They step up 1.5 nodes; the driver's jump key dashes (2.75 s to
recover). Left alone, a camel sits down to rest now and then. Cactus heals and breeds them.

**Sniffers** (rare in warm lands: the ocean ruins where Minecraft hides their eggs do not exist here) dig
up a patch of dirt or grass every 7-8 minutes and turn up **torchflower seeds** or a **pitcher pod**.
Planted on soil they grow into a **torchflower** (orange dye) and a **pitcher plant** (cyan dye).
Torchflower seeds breed sniffers: the pair lays a **sniffer egg**, which hatches after 20 minutes
(10 on mossy cobble).

**Golems you build:** a pumpkin (or jack-o'-lantern) placed on two snow blocks makes a **snow golem**, on a
copper block a **copper golem**, on four iron blocks in a T an iron golem; never where you may not build.
A snow golem throws snowballs at monsters (they only push back, but burn fiery mobs), leaves snow behind
in cool biomes outside protected areas and melts in hot ones and in water; shears take its pumpkin off.
Players have no snowballs. A copper golem sorts the chest it was built next to: it carries up to 16 of
one item to a chest within 16 nodes that already holds it, or else to an empty one (never a chest
protected against its builder). It oxidizes over hours of activity and finally stands still as a statue
until an axe scrapes a stage off; an empty hand makes it drop what it carries.

**Allays** live at pillager outposts (one per tower, back a day after it is gone). Give one an item and
it gathers the same items lying within 32 nodes and brings them to you, or to a note block (mesecons)
played near it in the last 30 s; an empty hand takes the item back. Its owner cannot hurt it.

## Water mobs (`cb_mobs_water`, needs mobs_redo)

The servers' fish, jellyfish, sharks, turtles and crocodiles (mobs_water) stay; these come on top.
**Squid** drift in open water and **glow squid** (they glow) in dark water underground; hit, they squirt
ink and dart away. Ink sacs make black dye, glow ink sacs cyan dye (glowing signs and frames do not
exist here). **Cod** school in the seas and **salmon** in cold waters; both flee from players, drop raw
fish and go into a water bucket and back. A **pufferfish** puffs up when a player or land mob comes close
and, fully puffed, stings what touches it with poison; eating one poisons and starves. **Dolphins** play in
warm seas: swimming near them gives Dolphin's Grace (faster swimming), and fed raw fish one leads you for
30 s towards the nearest structure. They breathe air and fight back as a pod. **Nautiluses** drift in
deeper warm water, charge whoever hurts them and sometimes drop a nautilus shell (taming, riding and
nautilus armour are not included). Fish, squid and nautiluses choke out of water.

**Ocean monuments** (`/cb_spawn ocean_monument` on deep sea, or `/cb_scatter ocean_monument N`) are built
of prismarine under the sea, on struts down to the sea floor, and kept by **guardians**: they aim a beam
for 2 s and then hurt (6), and prick back whoever hits them up close. Three **elder guardians** live in the
wings and the top room (rare: they come back a day after they are gone); they hurt more (8) and give
players within 50 nodes Mining Fatigue III, which all but stops digging for five minutes. New blocks:
prismarine, prismarine bricks, dark prismarine (stairs and slabs), sea lanterns, sponges (a dry sponge
soaks up the water around it; a wet one dries in a furnace).

## Nether mobs (`cb_mobs_nether`, needs mobs_redo)

They come in the Nether (our own on tiny, the `nether` mod's elsewhere). **Magma cubes** hop at players
and split into smaller ones; lava does not hurt them, and frogs eat the tiny ones (froglights).
**Ghasts** drift high and shoot fireballs that hurt and set things alight but break no blocks (fire only
in unprotected air); punch a fireball to send it back - a returned one kills a ghast. They drop ghast tears
and gunpowder. **Piglins** attack players who wear no gold armour (any 3d_armor piece with "gold" in its
name; on servers without gold armour they stay calm until hit); drop a gold ingot near a calm one and it
pays with an item from Minecraft's barter table (what the server has). **Zombified piglins** are calm,
but hit one and all within 32 nodes come for you. **Hoglins** attack players, avoid warped fungi and breed
on crimson fungi. Piglins and hoglins brought to the Overworld turn into zombified piglins and **zoglins**
(which attack everything) after 15 s. **Striders** walk over lava and shiver on land; saddle one and steer
it with a **warped fungus on a stick** (a fishing rod and a warped fungus). **Wither skeletons** live in
Nether fortresses; their blows wither (1 damage every 2 s for 10 s, it can kill), and they drop a wither
skeleton skull 1 time in 40. **Piglin brutes** (bastions only) attack every player in range, gold or
not, and take no gold.

**The Wither.** The skull is a block (on the floor or on a wall). Build a T of four soul sand or soul soil
blocks (the two spaces under the arms empty) and set three wither skeleton skulls on its arms, along x or
z, in any dimension: the T turns into the wither, unless the server is peaceful or a block of the T is
protected from the player. It grows for 10 s (unhurt), then blows up. It has 600 health, flies and, as
players have no ranged weapons, keeps 2 nodes above its target within sword reach, shooting skulls (8
damage and wither II for 10 s; every 4th is a slow blue one); its side heads shoot at other creatures
within 16. Below half health it puts on armour, comes down, blows up again and calls three wither
skeletons. Its blasts and skulls break blocks only where the player who summoned it may build, never
unbreakable ones (pocket barriers, portals, spawners); one broken block in four drops its item. Alone
(no player within 64) it heals 1 health a second. Players within 64 see its health bar at the top of the
screen. It drops a **nether star** and 50 experience; build another T to fight it again.

## The deep dark (`cb_mobs_deep`, `cb_blocks`)

**Deepslate** in all its Bedrock kinds: deepslate (drops cobbled deepslate, which smelts back), cobbled
and polished deepslate, deepslate bricks and tiles (with stairs, slabs and walls; four make the next
kind, cracked ones come from a furnace), chiseled deepslate (two cobbled deepslate slabs) and
**reinforced deepslate**, which nothing breaks. **Soul lanterns** (light 10: steel, a torch and soul soil
or soul sand), lit **candles** (one to four in a block; decor only, there is no beeswax to make them) and
the **skeleton skull** (floor or wall) decorate ancient cities.

**Sculk** blocks, veins, **sensors**, **shriekers** and **catalysts** drop no items (no silk touch here),
only experience (1 for sculk, 5 for the others). They only listen inside **ancient cities** (records of
the structure registry), so elsewhere they cost nothing: every 0.5 s a player there who moves without
sneaking wakes the sensors within 8, and a woken sensor, or stepping on a shrieker, makes the shriekers
within 8 shriek. A shrieker the city placed raises that player's **warning level** (at most once every
10 s, one level lost per 10 minutes) and gives **darkness** for 13 s to players within 40; at level 4 it
calls the warden. When a mob or player dies within 8 of a catalyst, up to 5 bare stone, dirt or
deepslate blocks around it (inside the city, never protected ones) turn to sculk.

The **warden** (500 HP, 30 a hit, no fire, lava or knockback) climbs out of the ground near the
shrieker, untouchable for 7 s, and digs back down after 60 s with nothing to disturb it; no other warden
comes within 48 of one. It is blind and hunts by **anger** kept per player or mob: +35 for each step it
hears (moving within 16, players only when not sneaking), +35 when it sniffs someone out (every 5-10 s,
within 6), +100 when hit, -1 a second. Below 80 it only walks to what it heard; at 80 it roars and hunts
the angriest one. Whoever it cannot reach gets the **sonic boom** (range 15, 20 up or down): 10 damage
past any armour. Players within 20 of it keep getting darkness. It drops a sculk catalyst and 5
experience. Like the other mobs_redo monsters it is not saved when its area unloads.

**Ancient cities** (`/cb_spawn ancient_city`, or `/cb_scatter ancient_city N`, which puts them at least
50 under the ground) are built in a cavern 134 nodes across, its floor at the given height when that is
-20 or lower, else at -51; a rim of deepslate seals it from caves, water and lava. Deepslate streets cross
it, sculk patches hold sensors and shriekers, and in the middle a stepped platform carries a reinforced
deepslate gate with a hidden vault under it. Around it stand ruined towers, broken walls, wool camps,
closed halls (with a shrieker and a sensor), ice stores, statues and basalt pillars. Chests hold
Bedrock's ancient city loot as far as the server has the items (echo shards, sculk, diamond leggings,
name tags, golden apples, books, coal and more); ice stores hold golden carrots, baked potatoes, ice and
snow. Nobody lives there: the warden comes only from the shriekers.

## Trial chambers (`cb_mobs_trial`, `cb_structures`)

**Trial chambers** (`/cb_spawn trial_chambers`, or `/cb_scatter trial_chambers N`, at least 40 under the
ground) are Mineclonia's corridors, chambers and atriums of tuff and copper, assembled underground: the
first corridor at the given height when that is -30 or lower, else at -40, nothing above -20. Nobody lives
there; mobs come from **trial spawners**. A trial spawner wakes when a player (not in creative) comes
within 14: it calls 6 mobs plus 2 for every further player, at most 2 plus 1 per further player at a time
(zombies, husks, spiders, slimes, silverfish, baby zombies, cave spiders, strays, skeletons, bogged or
breezes - each spawner its own kind). When the last one is gone every player of the trial gets a **trial
key** and some food, and the spawner sleeps for 30 real minutes (a restart does not reset it). A
**vault** takes a key and gives its loot once to each player: emeralds, iron, diamonds, golden carrots,
breeze rods, books, steel and diamond axes and chestplates, rarely a **heavy core** or a golden apple.
Only admins break spawners and vaults. Chests hold Bedrock's trial chamber loot (what the server has).
Copper and tuff blocks come with Bedrock's crafts (cut copper, grates, chiseled copper, bulbs - always
lit); copper does not oxidize here.

**Breezes** (30 HP) keep 4-8 nodes from their target, leap around it and fire wind charges: 1 damage and a
burst that throws creatures back and swings unprotected doors and trapdoors (no block breaks). Mob
projectiles do not hurt them. Killed by a player they drop 1-2 **breeze rods** and 10 experience.
**Bogged** (16 HP) are swamp skeletons whose arrows poison; shears take their mushrooms once. A heavy core
on a breeze rod makes the **mace**: 6 damage on the ground, much more after a fall (+4 a node for the first
3, +2 for the next 5, +1 beyond), and such a hit stops the fall without fall damage.

## Happy ghasts and desert undead (`cb_mobs_trial`)

A **dried ghast** (8 ghast tears around soul sand; piglins barter it too) placed next to water soaks up
one stage every 4-6 minutes and after the fourth hatches a **ghastling**; away from water it dries back. A
ghastling follows the nearest player and grows into a **happy ghast** in 20 minutes; snow (players have
no snowballs here) fed to it takes 2 minutes off each. A happy ghast (20 HP) drifts near its home. A
**harness** (3 leather over glass, wool and glass; 15 colours) put on with a right-click lets up to four
players ride (right-click to get on, sneak to get off); the first one steers along the look, jump rises.
It stands still while a player stands on its back, heals 1 HP every 30 s and takes no fall damage;
shears take the harness off.

**Parched** (16 HP) are desert skeletons whose arrows (mob projectiles only) weaken players for 11 s;
they do not burn by day. **Camel husks** (32 HP) come at desert nights with a husk in front, who steers
them at their target, and a parched behind; once the riders are gone a saddle makes one a camel to
drive (dash with jump) with a passenger seat. They drop 2-3 rotten flesh.

## Pale groves and creakings (`cb_blocks`, `cb_mobs_trial`, `cb_structures`)

There is no pale garden biome; **pale groves** are built by admins (`/cb_spawn pale_grove`, best in a
forest, or `/cb_scatter pale_grove N`): pale moss and moss carpets over the soil around the spot, pale
oaks (2x2 trunks, hanging moss under the leaves) and, in about one tree in ten (at least one per grove),
a **creaking heart**. **Pale oak** gives logs, planks with stairs, slabs, a fence and a door; its saplings
grow into pale oaks. Pale moss makes carpets.

A creaking heart between two pale oak logs along its axis sleeps by day and wakes at night; awake, with
a player within 32 it calls one **creaking** within 16. A creaking is calm until a player looks at it from
within 12; then it hunts that player, stands frozen while anyone looks at it and strikes (3) when nobody
does; it forgets its target beyond 24. While its heart stands nothing hurts it: blows make the heart
ooze 2-3 **resin clumps**. Breaking the heart makes its creaking crumble, as do dawn and wandering 34 nodes
from the heart. Hearts drop 1-3 resin clumps (a natural one also 20-24 experience). Resin: 9 clumps make a
block, a clump smelts into a resin brick, 4 bricks make resin bricks (stairs, slabs, wall, chiseled); two
logs around a block of resin make a new heart.

## Raids (`cb_raids`)

Killing a pillager captain (outposts) gives **Bad Omen**, one level more per captain up to V, for
100 minutes; captains of raid waves give none. A player with Bad Omen who enters a village built by
`/cb_spawn village` loses the effect and starts a raid there: 3 waves on easy, 5 on normal, 7 on hard
(`cubeborn.difficulty`), one more from Bad Omen II. Waves follow Bedrock's table (pillagers,
vindicators, ravagers, witches, evokers; a ravager's rider comes on foot), each with a captain; they
appear 24-40 nodes from the bell on solid ground, 15 s after the previous one is beaten, and walk to
the bell attacking villagers, golems and players. Ringing the bell lights raiders within 32 for 3 s.
Players within 64 see "Raid — wave N/M, K left" at the top of the screen. All waves beaten: **Hero
of the Village** for 40 minutes, at the raid's Bad Omen level (a trading discount). All villagers
dead, or 48 minutes without players near: the raid ends and the raiders become ordinary mobs. One raid
per village, at most `cubeborn.raid_max` (3) at once; raids and their raiders survive restarts.

Players are told along the way: Bad Omen comes with a sound and a chat line saying what it does; the
start of a raid is announced to the players near (and a horn sounds with every wave); a player whose
omen cannot start a raid learns why (no villagers in that village, or too many raids at once). Only
villages built by Cubeborn count, not houses of other mods.

- **Earning:** experience orbs drop from mobs killed by a player (monsters 5, animals 1-3, blaze 10,
  babies and villagers 0), from ores (coal 0-2, diamond and mese 3-7, quartz 2-5), from smelting
  (taken from the furnace, smoker or blast furnace), harvesting a ripe crop (1), fishing with the
  ethereal rod (1-6 per catch), breeding (1-7), every trade with a villager (3-6; not the banker's
  money exchange) and the ender dragon (12000 for the first kill, 500 later). Orbs fly to the nearest player within
  8 nodes, merge and vanish after 5 minutes.
- **Gear needs a level:** armour, shields, weapons and tools of every mod on the server are ranked
  (wood, stone, leather, clothes 0; steel, bronze, gold 5; silver 10; diamond, mese, mithril 15;
  crystal 20; nether 25; netherite, dragon, legendary armour, elytra 30; anything else by its
  strength, see `cb_xp/tiers.lua`). The tooltip shows the level. Armour above the player's level
  comes off into the inventory; weapons and tools above it hurt no one and dig nothing.
- **Repairs:** every anvil hammer blow costs 1 point (`cubeborn.xp_repair_cost`); without experience
  the anvil does not repair. Spending can drop the player below their armour's level.
- **Death:** a quarter of the experience is lost and drops as orbs at the place of death; admins
  (`cb_admin` or `server`) lose and drop nothing.
- **Leaderboard:** with `atl_server_statistics`, `/leaderboard` gets an Experience tab.

## Mobs (`cb_mobs_dim`, needs mobs_redo)

- Blaze (`cb_mobs_dim:blaze`): 20 HP, flies and shoots fireballs that set unprotected air on fire;
  fire and lava do not hurt it, water and snowballs do; drops 0-1 blaze rod. It does not spawn
  naturally, only from blaze spawners (`core.add_entity(pos, "cb_mobs_dim:blaze")`).
- Enderman (`cb_mobs_dim:enderman`): 40 HP, hits for 7, neutral until a player within 32 nodes looks
  at its head (or hits it). Projectiles do not hurt it: it teleports away (up to 32 nodes, onto
  unprotected ground, never through a pocket shell), as it does from water. It picks up natural grass,
  dirt, sand, gravel, clay, flowers and mushrooms outside protection and puts them down later; a carried
  block drops when it dies, stays carried over unloads and goes back when the enderman is removed. Drops 0-1 ender pearl. Spawns at night on the
  overworld surface (rare), in the Nether (rarer) and in the End (often). Spawn chances can be changed
  with the usual mobs_redo setting `cb_mobs_dim:enderman = <chance>,<count>`.
